# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze coffee shop sales performance. It provides insights into revenue trends, customer purchasing behavior, product performance, and store-level analysis.

The dashboard helps in identifying key business metrics and supports data-driven decision making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze overall sales performance
- Track key KPIs such as revenue, orders, and quantity sold
- Identify top-selling products and categories
- Compare weekday vs weekend performance
- Understand sales distribution across store locations
- Analyze hourly and daily sales patterns

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
- Power BI (Data Visualization & Dashboarding)
- Excel (Data Cleaning & Preparation)
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- Power Query (ETL Process)

---

## 📊 Key Metrics
- Total Sales: $157K
- Total Orders: 33,527
- Total Quantity Sold: 48,233
- Monthly Growth Analysis
- Weekday vs Weekend Sales

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features
- KPI Cards (Sales, Orders, Quantity)
- Sales Trend Analysis (Daily Sales)
- Sales by Product Category
- Sales by Product Type
- Sales by Store Location
- Weekday vs Weekend Analysis
- Hourly Heatmap for Sales Performance
- Month Filter (Dynamic)

---

## 🔍 Key Insights
- Weekdays contribute ~74% of total sales, while weekends account for ~26%
- Coffee is the top-performing category generating highest revenue
- Barista Espresso is the best-selling product type
- Peak sales hours are between 10 AM to 1 PM
- Store locations such as Hell's Kitchen and Astoria generate higher revenue
- Consistent growth observed compared to last month
